Why Choose Arton Automation

At Arton Robotics, we develop high-tech automation solutions that exceed expectations, blending innovation with technical maturity gained over nearly two decades of engineering and shop-floor experience at Toyota. We specialize in the integration and programming of highly complex industrial robots, working extensively with MIG welding, spot welding, painting robots, material handling (matehan), sealer/adhesive application, and SCARA robots. Driven by commitment and punctuality, we execute every project with complete responsibility and the strict production standards of automotive DNA, always prioritizing mutual respect and transparency with our clients, team members, and suppliers.
